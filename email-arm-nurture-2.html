In 2014 a psychiatrist named Bessel van der Kolk published a book that sat on the New York Times bestseller list for years. It’s called The Body Keeps the Score.
His thesis was simple. Trauma, stress, the long emotional arc of a life — these aren’t just stories the mind tells. They are patterns the body holds. And to move them, you have to work with the body, not just talk about it.
Years earlier, a researcher named Dr. Stephen Porges had introduced something called Polyvagal Theory — a framework for how the vagus nerve, the body’s primary parasympathetic pathway, regulates emotion, connection, and our sense of safety.
Today those ideas are no longer fringe. They’re taught in clinical psychology programs. They underpin somatic therapy, trauma-informed yoga, breathwork. They’re why Apple Watch, Oura and Whoop measure your Heart Rate Variability — a direct readout of how flexible your nervous system actually is.

The Alchemy Regulation Method sits on this science. Polyvagal theory, HRV, somatic practice, and the everyday physiology of breath, taught as protocols you can use on yourself.
