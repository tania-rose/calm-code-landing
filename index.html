What ARM Is

A methodology for building a nervous system
that doesn't just survive. It holds.

ARM is the methodology developed by Anthony Abbagnano and Amy Rachelle, founders of Alchemy of Breath. It draws on over two decades of breathwork practice, somatic science, and the direct results of working with 2,000+ practitioners across 40+ countries.

ARM offers a structured, repeatable path to nervous system regulation. Not as a temporary state. As a built capacity.

It is not therapy. It is not a bootcamp. It is a practical method, taught in your own body first, then in the bodies you work with.

The Framework

Six stages. One arc.
From descent to return.

The ARM Framework is a six-stage practice. Each stage has a job. Together they make the method.

01

Remember

Return to who you were before the dysregulation. Reconnect with the body's original intelligence. Beneath the noise, beneath the pattern.

02

Reframe

Change the story the nervous system is telling. Shift the meaning of past experience so the body stops bracing for a threat that has already passed.

03

Resource

Build internal capacity. Fill the tank. Learn to draw on breath, body, and community as living sources of regulation. Available anywhere, any time.

04

Regulate

Shift out of chronic activation. Learn the direct pathways from dysregulation to baseline. Through the breath, through the body, on demand.

05

Realign

Bring body, mind, and purpose back into coherence. When the nervous system is settled, clarity returns. And with it, the right direction.

06

Rise

Not just survival. Not just recovery. A robust nervous system becomes the foundation for vitality, leadership, and a life you can stand on.

"Not just survival. Not just recovery."

The Science

ARM works directly with the autonomic nervous system.

The biological architecture that governs how we respond to stress, threat, and safety. Through conscious breathwork, somatic practice, and structured regulation protocols, ARM targets the same systems that respiratory science measures.

The Program

Four pillars. One complete foundation.

40 residential hours at ASHA in Tuscany, plus a 10-hour online component you complete before arrival.

01

Pillar One

Mechanics

How the body is designed to breathe, and what gets in the way. Nasal function. Diaphragm assessment. Ribcage mobility. Posture.

02

Pillar Two

Chemistry

Why over-breathing creates dysregulation, and why CO2 tolerance matters more than most people realize. Taught simply, with safety first.

03

Pillar Three

Cadence

The rhythm and pacing of breath that shifts how the nervous system responds. Coherence, box breath, 4-7-8, transformer breath, and more.

04

Pillar Four

Application

Where understanding becomes capacity. The 90-second rapid reset. The 12-minute daily practice. Partner work. Holding space.

Right Fit

ARM is for you if...

  • ✓ You are carrying the weight of a dysregulated world. Anxiety, burnout, insomnia, reactivity.
  • ✓ You are a practitioner (coach, therapist, yoga teacher, healthcare pro) who wants to add breath to your work.
  • ✓ You want evidence-informed tools, not vague promises.
  • ✓ You are willing to do the work in your own body first.
  • ✓ You are open to a residential week in Tuscany.

Honest Check

ARM might not be the right fit if...

  • · You are looking for a weekend or fast-track certification.
  • · You cannot travel to ASHA for the residential week. The 10 online hours alone do not complete the training.
  • · You are looking for cathartic breathwork, trauma catharsis, or altered-state practices. Those live in different parts of our school.
  • · You need clinical diagnosis or treatment. ARM is educational, not medical.

ARM also works with organizations, facilitators, and retreat leaders. For team programs, keynotes, or hosting your community at ASHA, email hello@alchemyofbreath.com.
