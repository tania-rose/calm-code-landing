The 8-month journey

From your first conscious breath
to your certification day

Four hundred hours, carefully paced. Live calls every week, a digital learning platform you can return to forever, and mentors beside you the entire way.

I

Months 1 – 2 · Arrive

Foundations of the Breath

You begin with your own nervous system. The history and science of conscious connected breathing, the physiology of stress and release, and a daily personal practice that becomes your anchor for the months ahead.

  • Breath science & physiology
  • Nervous system regulation
  • Daily personal practice
  • Weekly live calls
II

Months 3 – 4 · Descend

Inner Alchemy

The work turns inward. Shadow work, inner-child inquiry, and trauma-informed awareness — rooted in depth psychology — because a facilitator can only guide others as far as they have traveled themselves.

  • Shadow work
  • Inner inquiry
  • Trauma-informed practice
  • Group process
III

Months 5 – 6 · Serve

The Art of Facilitation

Now you learn to hold the room. Creating a safe and loving container, the signature Mandala Breath method, working one-on-one and with groups, ethics, contraindications, and the subtle craft of presence.

  • Holding space
  • Mandala Breath method
  • 1:1 & group facilitation
  • Ethics & safety
IV

Months 7 – 8 · Rise

Practicum & Certification

Supervised practice sessions, written case studies, and personal mentorship carry you across the threshold. You graduate as a Certified Breathwork Facilitator — with the option to deepen at a 7-day residential BreathCamp in Tuscany.

  • Supervised case studies
  • Peer facilitation
  • Building your practice
  • Certification

What you'll actually learn

Application-focused and practical — every module pairs teaching with intensive exercises, so the knowledge lives in your hands, not just your notes.

Where modern neuroscience meets ancient practice. You'll understand precisely what happens in the body and brain during conscious connected breathing — and why it works.

  • The vagus nerve and polyvagal theory in practice

  • The lineages and history of breathwork

Learn to read states of activation and rest — in yourself first, then in the people you'll serve — and to guide a system gently back to safety.

  • Stress, trauma & the breath

Your guide

A lifetime of breath, distilled into your training

Anthony Abbagnano founded Alchemy of Breath in 2012, after a life-threatening illness revealed the breath as his way back to life. In the years since, he has guided breathwork everywhere from festivals and corporations to hospitals, prisons, and end-of-life care.

He serves on the board of the Global Professional Breathwork Alliance, and his weekly Breathe The World sessions have gathered hundreds of breathers across 40+ countries. Alongside co-founder Amy Rachelle and a team of master trainers, he teaches every cohort personally.
