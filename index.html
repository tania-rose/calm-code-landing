The Curriculum

Your 8-month journey, phase by phase

Four hundred hours of live teaching, practice, mentorship, and supervised facilitation — sequenced so that every skill rests on embodied experience, never on theory alone.

01Phase

Foundations of the Breath

The physiology, history, and lineages of conscious breathing. You build a daily personal practice and learn to read your own nervous system before you ever touch anyone else’s.

Months 1–2
02Phase

The Inner Alchemy

Deep personal process work: shadow, grief, forgiveness, and integration. Trauma-informed theory meets lived experience, because you can only take clients as deep as you’ve gone yourself.

Months 3–4
03Phase

The Art of Facilitation

Holding space, session architecture, voice and presence, working with emotional release, contraindications, and ethics. Supervised practice begins in small pods with Master Trainer feedback.

Months 5–6
04Phase

Practicum & Client Hours

Real sessions with real people — one-to-one and group — logged, reviewed, and mentored. You graduate having already facilitated, not hoping you’ll one day be ready.

Month 7
05Phase

Certification & The Business of Breath

Final assessment and your GPBA- and AADP-accredited diploma, plus positioning, pricing, and client-care frameworks to launch a practice that sustains you.

Questions, Answered

Everything people ask before the call

Do I need previous breathwork experience to join? +

No. The 8-month certification begins with foundations and builds progressively. Many of our most successful graduates began with nothing but their own breath and a calling to serve others.

Is the certification internationally recognised? +

Yes. The training is accredited by the Global Professional Breathwork Alliance (GPBA) and the American Association of Drugless Practitioners (AADP), with graduates practising in more than 60 countries.

How much time do I need each week? +

Plan for roughly 8 to 10 hours per week across live calls, practice sessions, and self-paced study. Live sessions are recorded, so the training fits around work and family life.

What happens on the discovery call? +

A relaxed 20-minute conversation with our admissions team. We learn about your intentions, answer every question about the curriculum, investment, and payment plans, and together decide whether this path is right for you. There is no pressure and no obligation.

Can I really build a career as a breathwork facilitator? +

Yes. The final phase of the training is dedicated to the business of breathwork: positioning, pricing, ethics, and client care. Graduates run private practices, retreats, corporate programmes, and online communities worldwide.
