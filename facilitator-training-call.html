Facilitator Training · The Next Step

You feel called to hold this work for others.

If you have come this far, the question of facilitating breathwork is no longer hypothetical. Something in you is asking whether this could become your path too.

Some applicants come ready to begin our facilitator training. Others find that deepening their own practice first, or starting with a shorter program, is the truer next step. Choose a time below and we will look at what fits where you are.

A short message before your call

The conversation runs for 30 minutes. Bring any questions about the training, the pace, what holding this work could look like for you, and anything still moving in you after the masterclass. Together we will look at where you are and what could be the right next step. If facilitator training is not the right fit right now, we will be honest, and point you toward what might be.
