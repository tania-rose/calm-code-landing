For years, many of us have told ourselves stories about who we are. Anxious. High-strung. Sensitive. Tired all the time.
What if those weren’t character traits? What if they were states — patterns held in the body, in a nervous system that’s been bracing for years without quite knowing how to stop?
That’s what the field of nervous system regulation has been pointing to. It’s what the Alchemy Regulation Method teaches — in your body, over seven days.
