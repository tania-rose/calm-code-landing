Why This Call Exists

Something in you is asking a question.
Let's make space to hear it.

Maybe you have been circling one of our programs for a while. Maybe you landed here after a friend mentioned a retreat. Maybe you are not sure what you are looking for yet, only that something has been calling.

Whatever brought you, the questions are real. Is this the right program for me? Am I ready? What if I choose wrong? Can I actually get what I need from this?

Those questions are not obstacles. They are the sign that you are taking this seriously. A short conversation can be the difference between years of circling and one honest yes or no.
