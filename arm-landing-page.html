A quiet check-in

How is your nervous system? Be honest.

You do not need a clinical reading to know. Your body has been telling you. Read these. Notice the ones that land.

  • Sleep is light or interrupted, even when you are tired.
  • Your jaw, shoulders or stomach is holding tension you cannot remember choosing.
  • Small things land bigger than they used to.
  • You cannot remember the last time you felt fully landed in your own body.
  • You think your way through stress more than you breathe through it.
  • You have read the books, downloaded the apps, and still feel underwater.

If two landed, this Sunday is for you. Most people we work with tick four.

By the end of Sunday

What you walk away with.

Not a vibe. Three measurable things you can take into Monday morning.

01 · The shift

A measurably more regulated nervous system.

HRV (heart rate variability) is the clinical marker of nervous system tone. Most people leave the Sunday with HRV 15-25% higher than they walked in. That is your system in a different gear, on the same day, without medication.

02 · The practices

Four daily breaths you can actually use.

One for the morning to set your baseline. One for the inbox-and-meeting middle of the day. One for acute stress, under sixty seconds. One for sleep wind-down. No equipment, no app, your breath only.

03 · The data

Three numbers you can read on yourself.

HRV at rest, breath cycles per minute, and recovery time after stress. You will know what to watch and what the numbers mean, so you can track your own regulation week to week without a clinic.

People leave reporting anxiety roughly half what they walked in with. Not a permanent state change in one day. What a held room, a working breath, and four practices can do in a single Sunday.

The Integration Pass is built for returners who want to stay close to the practice.

What is the difference between free and the Integration Pass?

Free gets you both live sessions on Sunday 21 June. The Pass adds the lifetime recording, 30 minutes of Q&A with Anthony and Amy after each session, and a private 7-day WhatsApp room with both of them in it. Same event. More of it.

How does the WhatsApp room work?

It opens on the morning of Sunday 21 June and closes seven days later, on 28 June. You get a join link in your welcome email. Anthony and Amy check in daily with voice notes, prompts, and answers. You bring questions as they come up while the practice is settling in your body.

I have never done breathwork. Is this for me?

It is. There is nothing to learn before Sunday. The whole point is that the room is held for you. Anthony or Amy guides every breath, and trained facilitators are there throughout. You do not need to have done the first Quantum Shift.

What if I cannot stay the full session?

Stay for as much as you can. If you have the Pass, the recording covers anything you miss, and you can bring questions into the WhatsApp room afterward.

What do I need to set up?

A quiet room, a mat or sofa to lie on, water, and headphones. Eat lightly an hour before. That is it.

What if my time zone does not match the session times?

You have two sessions to choose from, ten hours apart, so most time zones land at least one session at a workable hour. Both sessions are open to you with the same link.
