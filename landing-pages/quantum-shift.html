Three patterns

When a nervous system gets stuck, it usually gets stuck in one of three places.

See which one you recognize. Not to label yourself, but because the moment you can see the pattern clearly, it starts to lose its grip on you.

Pattern 1

The Pusher

You call it drive. Your body calls it survival mode. You can't quite stop. The silence feels dangerous. That isn't ambition. It's your nervous system holding the throttle down.

Pattern 2

The Thinker

The mental loop never stops. Analysis. Planning. Worry. You've built a fortress in your head because part of you doesn't trust what's underneath. And you're tired from the noise.

Pattern 3

The Withdrawer

You've gone quiet. Not dramatically, just slowly. The flatness. The "no." The handbrake pulled on your own life. Your nervous system isn't broken. It's protecting you from something it thinks is still there.

All three share one root. And all three can change the moment you give the body what it's actually been asking for.
