From Anthony

Why I am writing to you, and what I have learned in fourteen years of holding this room.

I started Alchemy of Breath fourteen years ago because of a question I could not put down. Why are so many of us bracing for something that is not here anymore? I had watched my own body do it. I had watched it in monks, in CEOs, in mothers, in healers who could regulate everyone in the room except themselves.

In the years since, I have sat with more than two thousand graduates as they learned to breathe their way back into their own bodies. I have noticed one thing, over and over. The nervous system does not respond to thinking. It responds to being met. No amount of insight will move a system that has not been met. And no amount of being met, on its own, will hold the change in place. It needs both.

The first Quantum Shift was the meeting. People came in tired, and many of them left with something that had not moved in years. Then most of them went straight back into the same week and watched it close again.

That is why I am running it a second time, on Sunday 21 June, and why this version comes with the option of a week in a small room with Amy and me on the other side. The Sunday is the door. The week that follows is the room. If you only have time for the door, come. The free seat is real, and the work in those two sessions stands on its own.

If you have been carrying something for a while and you know that one Sunday is not going to be enough, the Pass is the way I have built to keep my hand on your shoulder for the seven days the change is settling.

You do not need to be sick to need this. You just need to be tired of doing more for less. I would like you in the room on the 21st.

Anthony

What you carry home

Three things you will leave Sunday with.

Not a feeling that fades by Tuesday. Three things you can take into Monday morning and use on Friday night.

01 · The shift

A system that has answered.

Heart rate variability is the clinical signal of nervous system tone. After our Sundays, most people show their HRV measurably higher by the end of the day, in the range of fifteen to twenty-five percent. That is your system in a different gear, in real time, without medication.

02 · The breaths

Four practices, no app required.

One breath for the morning to set your baseline. One for the inbox and meetings middle of the day. One for acute stress, in under sixty seconds. One for sleep wind-down. Your breath only. You will know which one to reach for, and when, by Monday morning.

03 · The signals

Three numbers on your own body.

HRV at rest, breath cycles per minute, and how long it takes you to come back from a stress spike. I will teach you what to watch and what it means, so you can read your own regulation week to week without a clinic.

People leave reporting their anxiety roughly half what they walked in with. Not a permanent transformation in one day. What a held room, a working breath, and four practices honestly do in a single Sunday.

Honest answers

What people ask me before they say yes.

I came to the first Quantum Shift. Is this just a repeat?

No. The two live sessions are designed for both newcomers and returners, but the deepening is what happens on the other side: live Q&A with Amy and me after each session, and the seven-day WhatsApp room. If you came the first time and want what comes next, the Pass is built for you.

What is the actual difference between free and the Integration Pass?

Free gets you both live sessions on Sunday 21 June. The Pass adds the lifetime recording, thirty minutes of Q&A with Amy and me after each session, the seven-day WhatsApp room with both of us in it, and the Six-Stage Map of ARM I send ahead of the event. Same Sunday. More of it.

How does the WhatsApp room actually work?

It opens on the morning of Sunday 21 June and closes seven days later, on the 28th. You get the join link in your welcome email. Amy and I check in daily with voice notes, prompts, and direct answers. You bring questions as they come up while the practice is settling in your body. The room is capped at 25 voices because the conversation cannot stay real past that.

I have never done breathwork. Is this safe for me?

Yes. The whole point is that the room is held for you. Amy or I guide every breath, and trained facilitators are there throughout. There is nothing to learn beforehand and nothing to do other than turn up and lie down. You do not need to have done the first Quantum Shift.

What if I cannot stay for the full session?

Stay for as much as you can. If you have the Pass, the recording covers anything you miss, and you can bring questions into the WhatsApp room afterward. We will be there.

What if I take the Pass and it is not for me?

Sit through the first session. If by the end of it your body has not answered, write to me directly. I will refund you, no questions, and you keep the recording.

What do I need on Sunday?

A quiet room, a mat or a sofa, water, headphones. Eat lightly an hour beforehand. That is all.
