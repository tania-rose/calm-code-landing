A Note Before You Begin

There's nothing to do here.

If something opened up in our conversation, this is a quiet place to sit with it. Not a practice to perform, not a state to reach. Just a few minutes with Anthony, your breath, and whatever is alive in your chest right now.

Compassion, trust, and love are not things to build. They're already here. This meditation is an invitation to feel them, in your own body, in your own time.

"The heart does not need to be earned. It only needs to be remembered." Anthony Abbagnano

01

Before

Give yourself ten minutes longer than the video. Settle in. Drink some water. Let the day fall away for a moment.

02

During

There's nothing to get right. If your mind wanders, come back to the breath when you can. Tears are welcome. So is stillness.

03

After

Don't rush back into your day. Sit for a minute. Write a line in a notebook if anything wants to be said. The meditation continues for a while after the video ends.

Thank you for the conversation.

Come back to this meditation whenever you need it. It's yours.
